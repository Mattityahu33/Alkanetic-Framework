## Purpose

The registry is the master index of all standards, templates, SOPs, examples, guides, checklists, reference documents, and architecture decisions in the Alkanetic Framework.

It enables discoverability across the entire framework and provides a single location where any document can be found by type, status, or category.

---

## Scope

Every document that reaches `approved` status must be registered here.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Index files per document type (standards, templates, SOPs, etc.)
- Cross-reference tables linking related documents
- Registry maintenance guidelines

---

## What Does Not Belong Here

- The documents themselves (they live in their home sections)
- Draft or in-progress documents (register only on approval)
