# Standard Operating Procedures

**Status:** Scaffold
**Owner:** Alkanetic Engineering
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-02

---

## Purpose

This section contains the standard operating procedures (SOPs) that define step-by-step processes for recurring engineering and operational activities at Alkanetic.

---

## Scope

SOPs in this section apply to all engineers and team members executing recurring processes. Each SOP defines who performs the task, when it is performed, and the exact steps to follow.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Engineering SOPs (e.g., incident response, release process, environment provisioning)
- Business analysis SOPs (e.g., requirements sign-off, stakeholder review)
- Project delivery SOPs (e.g., project kick-off, retrospective)
- Onboarding SOPs
- Quality review SOPs

---

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Standards and principles (see `../engineering-standards/`, `../architecture/`)
- Templates and checklists (see `../templates/`, `../checklists/`)
- Governance policies (see `../governance/`)
