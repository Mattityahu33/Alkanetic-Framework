# Business Analysis

**Status:** Scaffold
**Owner:** Alkanetic Business Analysis
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-02

---

## Purpose

This section defines the business analysis standards, methodologies, and artefacts used by Alkanetic to gather, document, validate, and manage requirements for all projects.

---

## Scope

Applies to all business analysts, product owners, and engineers involved in requirements gathering, user story creation, process mapping, and stakeholder management.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Requirements gathering standards
- User story and acceptance criteria standards
- Process mapping and workflow documentation standards
- Stakeholder management guidelines
- Business analysis artefact templates (see `../templates/` for the templates themselves)
- BA review and validation procedures

---

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Architecture decisions (see `../architecture/`)
- Project management processes (see `../project-management/`)
- Engineering implementation standards (see `../engineering-standards/`)
- Specific project documentation (project repositories)
