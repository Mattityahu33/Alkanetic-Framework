# Design Standards

**Status:** Scaffold
**Owner:** Alkanetic Design
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-02

---

## Purpose

This section defines the UI/UX design principles, component standards, and visual language guidelines that ensure consistency and quality across all Alkanetic-delivered products.

---

## Scope

Applies to designers, frontend engineers, and product leads involved in creating or reviewing user interfaces and user experience flows for Alkanetic projects.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Design principles and philosophy
- Colour, typography, and spacing systems
- Component design standards
- Accessibility and inclusive design standards
- Design review criteria
- Design handoff standards (design-to-engineering workflow)
- Brand alignment guidelines (see also `../assets/branding/`)
- Responsive and mobile-first design standards

---

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Brand logo and visual assets (see `../assets/`)
- Engineering implementation of components (see `../engineering-standards/`)
- Project-specific design files (project repositories)
