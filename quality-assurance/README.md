# Quality Assurance

**Status:** Scaffold
**Owner:** Alkanetic Engineering
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-02

---

## Purpose

This section defines the quality assurance processes, standards, and metrics that ensure Alkanetic software and framework documents meet defined quality thresholds before delivery.

---

## Scope

Applies to all engineers and QA practitioners involved in testing, review, and validation activities across projects and the framework itself.

---

## What Belongs Here

- QA process standards
- Testing strategy standards (unit, integration, end-to-end, regression)
- Test coverage requirements
- Bug classification and triage standards
- Quality metrics and reporting
- QA review checklists coordination (see also `../checklists/`)
- Non-functional quality standards (performance, reliability, accessibility)

---

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Engineering coding standards (see `../engineering-standards/`)
- Deployment procedures (see `../deployment/`)
- Security testing specifics (see `../security/`)
- Individual project test plans (project repositories)
