# Templates

**Status:** Scaffold

---

## Purpose

This section stores all reusable document and artefact templates used across the Alkanetic Framework and associated projects.

---

## Scope

Templates in this section are used by engineers, business analysts, architects, and project managers when creating new framework documents or project artefacts.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Document templates (README templates, report templates)
- Engineering artefact templates (ADR templates, technical spec templates)
- BA artefact templates (user story templates, requirements templates)
- Project management templates (project brief, retrospective)
- Meeting note templates
- Review templates (code review, architecture review)

---

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Completed documents (those live in their respective sections)
- Checklists (see `../checklists/`)
- Examples of completed artefacts (see `../examples/`)
