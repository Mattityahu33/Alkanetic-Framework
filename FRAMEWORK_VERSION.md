## Versioning Policy

The Alkanetic Framework uses semantic versioning adapted for documentation:

- **Major (X.0.0):** Structural reorganisation or framework-wide paradigm change.
- **Minor (0.X.0):** New sections added or significant content milestones reached.
- **Patch (0.0.X):** Updates to individual documents, corrections, or minor additions.
