# Code of Conduct

**Owner:** Alkanetic
**Status:** Active

---

## Our Commitment

Alkanetic is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and collaborative environment for all contributors to this framework repository.

---

## Expected Behaviour

- Communicate professionally and constructively.
- Provide and receive feedback with respect.
- Prioritise the integrity and quality of the framework.
- Follow contribution guidelines as defined in [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Escalate disagreements through appropriate channels rather than unilateral changes.

---

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Disrespectful or dismissive communication.
- Unauthorised deletion or modification of approved framework documents.
- Bypassing the review process for significant changes.
- Introducing content that conflicts with approved standards without prior discussion.

---

## Enforcement

Violations should be reported to the framework owner. Repeated violations may result in restricted repository access.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions within this repository including pull requests, issues, comments, and commit messages.
