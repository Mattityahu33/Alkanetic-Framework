# Project Management

**Status:** Scaffold
**Owner:** Alkanetic Delivery
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-02

---

## Purpose

This section defines the project management standards, delivery frameworks, and reporting guidelines used by Alkanetic to plan, execute, monitor, and close software projects.

---

## Scope

Applies to project managers, delivery leads, and engineers involved in project planning, estimation, sprint management, and stakeholder communication.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Project delivery framework (methodology overview)
- Project initiation standards (kick-off, scoping, project brief)
- Estimation and planning standards
- Sprint and iteration management standards
- Stakeholder reporting standards
- Risk and issue management standards
- Project close and retrospective standards

---

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Business analysis standards (see `../business-analysis/`)
- Engineering implementation standards (see `../engineering-standards/`)
- Project-specific plans and documents (project repositories)
