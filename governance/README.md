---

## Purpose

This section defines the governance structures, policies, review processes, and approval workflows that ensure the Alkanetic Framework remains accurate, consistent, and authoritative.

---

## Scope

Governance documents apply to all framework contributors and to decisions that affect the structure, standards, or policies of the Alkanetic Framework.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Framework ownership and decision authority
- Document approval and review processes
- Change management policies
- Compliance requirements
- Roles and responsibilities within the framework
- Escalation paths and conflict resolution

---

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Engineering implementation standards (see `../engineering-standards/`)
- SOPs for day-to-day engineering tasks (see `../sop/`)
- Project management processes (see `../project-management/`)
